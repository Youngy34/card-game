# Option B (turns, two actions, footwork, new knockdown): predictions and pass bars

Written 18 November, before any sim run.

## What B is, as built
- Only the initiative holder of the round takes the end-of-round turn, then the token passes as before.
- Two actions per turn from the usual menu (move with 2 Charge, Overdrive, charge up, Zone) plus footwork:
  move by discarding a Guard. At most one move (either kind) and one zone per turn; any action may be taken twice otherwise.
- Overdrive taken once covers your next clash; taken as both actions (4 Charge), your next two clashes.
- Knockdown: your next turn has one action, and you lose every speed clash in your next clash.

## Reasoning first
- Turns plus footwork mean whoever has the turn can almost always set the range for the next clash: they
  hold 2-4 Guards most rounds. Expect range to become a rhythm: each clash after your turn at your range.
  Stuck spells should nearly vanish (one clash at the weak range at most, when the opponent moves you).
- The cost is a Guard per move, so hands shrink and players Guard less in the clash: faster games.
- The clash after your turn is your range plus maybe Overdrive, but the opponent wins speed ties there.

## Predictions (from the idea doc, plus mine)
- Brawler's 2-or-fewer-choice clashes at Far: 29% -> about 15%. Longest stuck spells shrink (mean away spell under 1.5).
- Range changes per game: about 4 -> 6-9. (Mine: likely at the top of that or above; flips most turns.)
- Swing: up 2-5 points.
- Time-outs: flat to down 3.
- Balance (Brawler v Zoner): within 3 points of today's.
- First player (initiative holder in round 1) wins 50-55%.
- Overdrive buys rise; never Overdriving costs more than today's 3.6 points.

## Pass bars
- Brawler 2-or-fewer at Far at or under 20%.
- Swing no more than 6 points above today's (same partner).
- Time-outs no more than 3 points above today's.
- Balance 44-56%.
- First player at or under 57%.
- Ladder: Medium against Hard not above 40% (skill not lost).
- Ping-pong: report how often a move is answered by a move straight back; a fail if it's most of them
  AND range changes go above 10 a game (range stops being a position at all).

# Option A (today's rules plus footwork), today's partner only: predictions (18 Nov, 20:18, before the run)
- Hands shrink as in B (about 0.5-0.7 cards at the clash), so Brawler at Far ≤2 choices rises from 30% to about 38-44%.
- More ping-pong than B: moves answered straight back above B's 34-38% (today 28%).
- Stuck spells shrink less than under B (3+ spells about 20-25%, B 16-17%).
- Never using footwork costs Hard little (within 3 points).
- Pass bar as for B: Brawler at Far ≤2 choices at or under 20%.
