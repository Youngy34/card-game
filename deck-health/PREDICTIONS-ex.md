# EX pairs: predictions and pass bars (written before the run, 24 Nov 2026, ~22:05)

## The rule (v0 plus this)

- **Instead of one card, you may play two cards of the same type together** (an EX).
- **The pair counts as one move,** with the faster card's speed and the heavier card's +x.
- **Against the same type:** an EX beats any single card. EX against EX: the faster wins; equal speed is a tie.
- **Against other types,** the triangle decides as usual.
- **The extra card is always lost:**
    - win with an EX: take back one of the two (your choice, shown) and discard the other;
    - lose with an EX: discard both, plus the winner's +x.
- Discards still come from hand, your choice.

## Pass bars

- **A decision, not a reflex:**
    - EX share 10–25%;
    - never using EX costs 3–10 points;
    - always using it costs ≥3 points.
- **Skill not worse:** random v Greedy ≤20%, thumb ≤30%.
- **Length** ≤ v0 (15.3).
- **Seat** 47–53%.

Confidence: about 65% that it comes out a decision; the main risk is that it's a reflex in mirrors you can read.
